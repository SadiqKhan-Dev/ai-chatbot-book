## Overview

This chapter introduces the fundamental concepts of Physical AI and explains why embodiment is crucial for intelligent systems.

## What is Physical AI?

Physical AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that are embodied in physical form - typically robots - and interact with the real world through sensors and actuators.

### Key Characteristics

- **Embodiment**: The AI has a physical body
- **Sensory Input**: Receives information from the environment
- **Actuation**: Can affect the physical world
- **Real-time Processing**: Must respond quickly to environmental changes

## Why Embodiment Matters

Traditional AI systems (like LLMs) operate in disembodied digital spaces. Physical AI systems must:

1. **Navigate uncertainty** - The physical world is unpredictable
2. **Handle noise** - Sensor data is inherently noisy
3. **Act under constraints** - Physical limitations on movement and power
4. **Learn from interaction** - Direct feedback from actions
