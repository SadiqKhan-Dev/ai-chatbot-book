# Introduction to Physical AI

Welcome to Module 1 of the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics course. This module introduces the foundational concepts of embodied intelligence and physical AI systems.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

- Understand the difference between disembodied and embodied AI
- Explain the importance of physical embodiment in robot intelligence
- Describe key concepts in situated AI and sensorimotor learning
- Identify the components of a physical AI system

## Topics Covered

1. **What is Physical AI?** - Understanding embodied intelligence
2. **History of Robotics** - From early automatons to modern humanoids
3. **Embodiment and Situated AI** - Why physical form matters
4. **Sensorimotor Learning** - How robots learn from interaction
5. **Case Studies** - Real-world physical AI applications

## Prerequisites

- Basic understanding of Python programming
- Familiarity with linear algebra concepts
- No prior robotics experience required
