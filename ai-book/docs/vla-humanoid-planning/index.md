# Module 4: VLA Humanoid Planning

Enable humanoid robots to understand human commands and execute multi-step tasks through Vision-Language-Action pipelines and LLM-driven planning.

---

## Learning Objectives

- Understand VLA pipeline architecture for humanoid robots
- Convert voice commands into structured robot intents
- Use LLMs for cognitive task planning over ROS 2
- Integrate perception for closed-loop execution

## Prerequisites

- Module 1: ROS 2 & URDF Fundamentals
- Module 2: Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)
- Module 3: NVIDIA Isaac Sim & Nav2

## Estimated Time

13 hours across 3 chapters

## Chapters

### Chapter 1: Voice to Action Pipelines

Speech-to-text, intent classification, and command parsing for humanoid robots.

### Chapter 2: Cognitive Planning with LLMs

LLM-driven task decomposition and ROS 2 action bridging.

### Chapter 3: Capstone - The Autonomous Humanoid

End-to-end VLA integration with perception feedback.
